# Changelog

What changed in each version of Handbeat. Newest first. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## 1.0.0

The first release.

### Groove box

- Sixteen pads with a step page, a sound page and a pattern page, one shared tempo, swing and transport.
- Sixteen patterns per project, chained into songs of up to 99 bars.
- Live recording from the pads, with COPY and ERASE on the pattern page.
- Sixteen live effects played over the whole mix by holding a pad.

### Devices

- Eight kinds of device in a rack of sixteen: Drums, Sampler, Bass, Pluck, Pad, Chip, Vocal and Acid.
- A mixer with a volume for every device and a main volume, a master limiter on the output.
- Record your own sounds with the microphone or import audio files into most devices.

### Projects

- Several projects, saved automatically.
- A demo project with fourteen devices and a 40-bar song.
- Share a project as a `.handbeat` file and import it back, with its own sounds.
- Export a project as WAV or M4A.

### App

- Themes: Classic, iClassic, Contrast and Aero, each in dark, light and system modes, and a choice of app icons.
- Menus and popups in eleven languages: English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Português, Italiano, Русский, Srpski, 日本語, 한국어 and 简体中文.
- A short onboarding at the first launch.
- iPhone and iPad, with VoiceOver labels for the pads, knobs and display.
- This guide in every language, a privacy policy and a way to write to us from Settings.
