# Handbeat privacy policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

**The short version: Handbeat collects nothing about you.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your music anywhere on its own.

## What stays on your device

Everything you make lives **on your iPhone or iPad**:

- **Projects**: your patterns, settings and the sounds you recorded or imported are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
- **Settings**: your language, theme, app icon and other choices are saved on your device.

None of it is sent to me or to any third party. If you delete the app, it goes with it.

## The microphone

Handbeat asks for the microphone only so you can **record your own sounds**. It listens **only while you hold the record button**, and the recording is saved into your project on your device. It is not uploaded, analysed or shared.

You can allow or take back the permission at any time in the Settings app of your phone, under Handbeat.

## Files and sharing

When you **export** audio or **share** a project, Handbeat gives the file to the system share sheet, and *you* choose where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail and so on). Handbeat doesn't see or control what happens to it after that. When you open a `.handbeat` file in the app, it is only read on your device.

## Third parties

Handbeat contains no third-party code, no advertising or analytics services, and does not connect to the internet by itself.

The **Guide** and **Privacy policy** rows in Settings open web pages in your browser. They are hosted on GitHub, so GitHub's own privacy policy applies to visiting them.

If you chose to share app analytics with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give me anonymous, aggregated usage and crash statistics. I can't identify you from them.

## Contacting me

If you write to [handbeat.contact@gmail.com](mailto:handbeat.contact@gmail.com), I receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. I use them only to reply to you and to improve Handbeat, and I don't share them. Ask me and I will delete your messages.

## Children

Handbeat doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
